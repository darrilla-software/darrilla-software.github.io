MonkeyRilla is in development. This page describes the current banana-gathering
playtest, ahead of a store release. Goals, pacing and presentation may change.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=MonkeyRilla%20support).
Include your app version, device, operating-system version, level and what
happened. A screenshot or short recording helps us see movement or terrain
problems. Leave out private information; never send passwords or payment details.

## How do I move and collect?

Touch and drag on the playing field to steer; release to stop. The view follows
your monkey around a larger map. Walk into bananas to collect them. A single
counts one, a normal bunch three and a larger bunch six. You should not have to
place the monkey exactly over a banana to collect it.

## When does a level finish?

Reach the banana target before the timer runs out. Meeting the target ends the
round immediately. If time runs out first, retry the current level. Collected
bananas still count toward lifetime unlocks. Winning advances the journey;
earlier completed levels are not selectable in this playtest.

## How do monkeys unlock?

Lifetime bananas unlock Fast at 250, Climber at 500 and Ninja at 750, including
bananas from failed attempts. An unlock card explains the helper; its portrait
then appears at the top. Unlocks are permanent and do not spend your bananas.
Previously earned monkeys stay owned when you update.

## Why does a helper need to rest?

Baby is your permanent monkey. Select an unlocked helper for up to ten seconds,
or switch back sooner. Each second selected needs two seconds of rest for Fast,
three for Climber and four for Ninja. For example, use Climber for one second
and he rests for three. Helpers recover independently while you use Baby or
another available monkey.

## How do I reach the upper jungle?

Rock-sided upper areas begin in later levels after Climber unlocks. Select
Climber to scale the rock. Once on top, switch to Baby and roam freely to collect
while Climber rests; use Climber again to descend. These levels need some upper
bananas to reach the target.

If Climber's time runs out on a rock face, Baby holds safely at that height.
Wait for Climber to recover and select him to finish the climb. You do not fall
or receive a free climb as Baby.

## What does Ninja's Dash do?

Select Ninja, steer toward the direction you want and tap **Dash**. The short
burst can cross thorn vines and collect bananas; Dash needs two seconds before
another burst. Ninja still rests after his assist. A thorn shortcut is optional:
you can walk around its ends. Dash cannot cross water or climb a rock face.

## What about ponds, rivers and snowbanks?

Walk around ponds and snowbanks. Cross a river on its wooden bridge. Obstacles
do not cause damage or sliding. If artwork appears walkable but stops you, or a
banana appears within reach but cannot be collected, please send the level and
a screenshot. That is useful playtest feedback.

## How do I pause or change sound?

**Pause → Settings** has separate Music and Sound effects On/Off controls and
volume sliders, plus **Test sound**. You can also open Settings from the title.
Music previews there while movement, the level timer and helper timers stay
paused. Calm motion steadies the camera and holds character poses.

## Where is my progress saved?

Your current stage, banana totals, earned monkeys, records and preferences are
stored locally. Updates are designed to retain them. Unreadable progress shows
an error or recovers a retained backup; it should not silently start a new game.
There is no game account or account-based cloud sync.

If progress looks wrong, stop and contact us with the message shown. **Do not
uninstall or clear app data to troubleshoot**: that can remove local saves.
Device backup settings are separate from the game's own recovery copies.

Current development builds include **Pause → Restart from beginning…** for
deliberate testing. It requires confirmation and keeps recovery copies, but it
starts gathering again with Baby and zero gathering progress. It is not a
routine troubleshooting step.

## Where can I download it?

Use the [MonkeyRilla Android testing link](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.monkeyrilla)
with the invited Google account. The first release is submitted for review;
installation is available after approval. See the [enrollment steps](/testing/).
For iPhone/iPad, use your personal TestFlight invitation. External beta access
also depends on Apple’s review. No public store release is available yet.

A Google Play build may not update an earlier locally installed test build with
a different signing key. If an update is incompatible, contact us; **do not
uninstall or clear data**, because that can remove progress.
