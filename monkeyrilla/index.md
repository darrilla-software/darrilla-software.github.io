## One more banana. One more jungle.

Drag to move around a large map while the view follows your monkey. Gather
individual bananas, three-banana bunches and occasional bigger bunches worth
six. Reach the level's target before time runs out and the round ends with a win.
Failed attempts still count toward lifetime banana unlocks.

The current development game has **40 forward-only jungle levels**, with sunny,
mossy, twilight, autumn and winter settings. Later routes add raised rock jungles,
ponds, wooden bridges, snowbanks and thorn-vine shortcuts. Targets and difficulty
are still being refined through playtesting.

## A little help from the crew

Baby is always ready. Three permanent unlocks add short assists:

- **Fast — 250 lifetime bananas:** a burst of faster movement.
- **Climber — 500:** scale rock faces to reach bananas in the upper jungle.
- **Ninja — 750:** steer and dash through thorn-vine shortcuts.

Assists last up to ten seconds, then return you to Baby while the helper rests.
Switch back early to shorten that rest. Gather upstairs as Baby while Climber
recovers, then call on Climber to come down. Water still needs a bridge, and
thorn vines also have a route around them.

## Made for a little exploring

Pause for a break or adjust Music, Sound effects and Calm motion in Settings.
Progress and settings stay on your device. The current game works offline,
without an account, ads or purchases.

The icon above is the game's Baby Monkey artwork, not a gameplay screenshot.
