Last updated: October 25, 2026

MonkeyRilla is a jungle game made by Darrilla Software. This policy describes
the current banana-gathering test game. Closed testing has been submitted to
Google Play and Apple TestFlight; this is not a public store release.

## Playing the game

MonkeyRilla works offline. It does not require an account or ask for your name,
email address, contacts or location to play. The current game does not send
banana totals, progress or usage data to a Darrilla server.

The current build has no advertising, purchases, chat, user uploads or analytics
SDKs. Unity's connected analytics, advertising and cloud diagnostic services are
disabled. Music and sound are bundled with the game and play locally; they are
not streamed from an online music service.

## Data on your device

The game stores banana totals, earned monkeys, the current stage, completed-level
records and settings such as Music, Sound effects, volume and Calm motion.
Previous adventure progress and save-recovery copies may also remain on-device
when the game is updated. These support continuity and recovery, not advertising
or a Darrilla player profile.

The game has no account-based cloud-sync service. Your device or operating-system
backup settings may handle app data separately. Clearing app data or removing
the app can remove saves, preferences and local recovery copies.

## Testing and app stores

The game does not use a game login. Google Play and Apple TestFlight handle tester accounts,
installation and their own diagnostics under their own policies. If you choose
to send feedback or crash reports through a platform, Darrilla Software may
receive the report to investigate the problem. Any such testing link will be
listed on this site when available. The [Android closed-test link](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.monkeyrilla)
requires an invited account and an approved active release. See [testing help](/testing/).

## Children and families

The current game does not ask for a player's age or collect personal information
from children to play. It has no chat, ads or purchases. A parent or guardian can
contact us about personal information sent directly to support.

## Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever information you
choose to send, such as a device model, screenshot or bug description. We use
it to answer the request and investigate the issue, and retain it as needed for
those purposes. Contact us about information you have sent directly to us.
Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

## This website

These pages do not add an analytics tracker. They are hosted on GitHub Pages,
which logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. This is separate from
the offline game. See [GitHub's Pages data-collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes and contact

If a future version changes how information is handled, this page will be
updated with a revised date. Services used by another Darrilla game are not
therefore included in MonkeyRilla.

For privacy questions or requests about support information, email
[darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=MonkeyRilla%20privacy).
