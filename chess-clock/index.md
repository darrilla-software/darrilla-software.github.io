## Your next game, ready in seconds

- Choose a preset such as **3+2**, **5+0**, or **10+0**, or set your own time.
- Add Fischer increment and save favorite setups for next time.
- Follow each player's remaining time and move count.
- Pause, resume, or restart from the play surface.
- Hear turn handoff and flag-fall sounds.

The core clock works offline, with no account or login. Basic timing and
Fischer increment are free.

## More control with Pro

Pro is an optional **one-time purchase**, with no subscription. It adds:

- Simple delay and Bronstein delay.
- Different time controls for White and Black.
- Multi-stage tournament controls.

Give a newer player extra time or practice a tournament schedule. If you've
already bought Pro, use **Restore Purchases** with the same store account.

## Keep your attention on the board

An active game pauses when the app loses focus. The app's interruption
protection guide explains device settings that can reduce distractions during
play. Check your setup before a timed match.
