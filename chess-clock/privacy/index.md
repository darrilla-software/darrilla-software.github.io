Last updated: October 25, 2026

Darrilla Chess Clock is a mobile chess clock app designed for over-the-board
play, made by Darrilla Software.

## Information We Collect

The core clock runs on your device and does not send game timing or saved
preferences to a Darrilla server.

The app does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your
name, email address, phone number, location, or contacts.

Chess Clock does not collect your payment information directly. If you choose
to buy an optional in-app purchase, payment processing is handled by the
platform app store.

## How the App Works

Chess Clock runs locally on your device. Game timing, settings, and app
behavior are handled on-device.

Saved time controls and preferences are stored locally on your device.

Optional Pro purchases and restores use the platform app store. The app and
purchasing library handle purchase records and entitlement status to unlock
or restore Pro; Darrilla Software does not receive your card details.

The app has no account-based cloud sync. Device backup settings may handle local
app data separately. Clearing data or removing the app can remove preferences.

## Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

Chess Clock does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking
technologies.

## Data Sharing

Darrilla Software does not sell local timing or preference data. App stores
process purchase and testing information separately, as described below.

## Support Messages

If you email Darrilla Software, we receive your email address and any information
you choose to include. We use this information to respond to your request and
investigate the issue. Please do not include passwords or payment details.

## App Stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store handle downloads, purchases, and purchase
restores under their own privacy policies. Their handling of store account and
payment information is separate from the local clock.

## Closed Testing

[Chess Clock's Google Play closed test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.dburns.chess_clock)
is separate from the public download. Google handles tester-account eligibility
and distribution. Google Play and Apple may process installation, usage or
diagnostic information under their own platform policies. If you send beta
feedback or crash reports through a platform, Darrilla Software may receive
them to investigate the problem. This is separate from the clock's local timing.

## This Website

These pages do not add an analytics tracker. GitHub Pages hosts the website and
logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes. See
[GitHub's Pages data-collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and [privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Children's Privacy

Chess Clock does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

If Chess Clock's features change in the future, this Privacy Policy may be
updated. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised
"Last updated" date.
