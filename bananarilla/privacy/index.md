Last updated: November 6, 2026

BananaRilla: Jungle Defense is made by Darrilla Software. This policy describes
the current offline development game, including Adventure and Workshop.

## Playing the game

The current game works offline and does not require a player account. It does
not ask for your name, email address, contacts or location to play, or send
gameplay progress or usage analytics to a Darrilla server.

The current build has no ads, in-app purchases, chat, user uploads or analytics
SDKs. Unity's connected analytics, advertising and cloud diagnostic services are
disabled. Game artwork and sound are bundled locally.

## Data on your device

BananaRilla stores its current runs, defender purchases and upgrades, coins,
bananas, round progress, best results and preferences such as Sound and Calm
motion locally. Adventure and Workshop have separate saves. Earlier development
versions and recovery snapshots may remain on the device to preserve runs.

There is no game account or account-based cloud sync. Your device's backup
settings may handle app data separately. Removing the app or clearing its data
can remove local saves and settings.

## Testing and diagnostics

Local Android testing continues, and internal Apple TestFlight invitations
have been issued. Google Play closed testing and external TestFlight beta
access have not been submitted for review. The [Android test page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.bananarilla)
will provide access only after approval and release. These platforms handle
their own accounts, installation and platform diagnostics under their policies.
Reports you choose to share through a testing platform may be made available
to Darrilla Software to investigate problems.

## Children and families

The current game does not ask for age or collect personal information from
children to play. It has no ads, purchases or chat. A parent or guardian can
contact us about information sent directly to support.

## Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose
to send, such as a device model, screenshot or bug report. We use it to respond
and investigate the issue, and retain it as needed for those purposes. Contact
us about information you have sent directly to us. Please do not send passwords,
payment details or other sensitive information.

## This website

These pages do not add an analytics tracker. GitHub Pages hosts this website
and logs visitor IP addresses for security purposes, separately from the offline
game. See [GitHub's Pages data-collection information](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection)
and [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Changes and contact

We will update this policy if a future version changes how information is
handled. Services in another Darrilla game do not automatically apply to
BananaRilla.

For questions or requests about support information, email
[darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=BananaRilla%20privacy).
