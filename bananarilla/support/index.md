BananaRilla: Jungle Defense is in development. This guide describes the current
30-round Banana Grove Commons Adventure and the separate Workshop sandbox.
Features and balance may change before release.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=BananaRilla%20support).
Include your app version, device, operating-system version, round, and whether
you were in Adventure or Workshop. Tell us what happened and what you expected.
A screenshot or short recording helps; leave out private information.

## How do I place a defender?

Open **Adventure** and choose **Play** or **Resume**. Drag a portrait from the
sidebar onto clear ground, or tap the portrait and then tap a legal position.
The placement preview shows where the defender can go. You need enough Jungle
Coins, and cannot place on the trail, scenery or another defender.

Scroll or use the page arrows to see more defenders. Tap a placed defender to
select it and see its range, upgrades and targeting controls. Tap it again to
deselect it.

## How do rounds and bananas work?

Press **Play** to begin the next round when you're ready. Thieves travel to the
cache, take bananas and head back toward the entrance. Catch loaded thieves
before they escape to recover their cargo. An empty cache does not mean all
bananas are lost if thieves are still carrying some you can recover.

Captures earn Jungle Coins. Clear all 30 rounds with bananas remaining to win;
the best medal and banana result stay saved. **Replay** starts another attempt
while keeping that best result. After a loss, **Retry** starts a fresh attempt.

## How do upgrades and powers work?

Select a placed defender and buy its next upgrade. Each defender has ten ordered
upgrades that build on earlier abilities, with new character artwork along the
way. Special powers activate automatically when ready and a suitable target is
available. You do not need to tap an ability button.

The circular **i** button changes with your selection: a roster defender shows
its full progression, a placed defender shows its next upgrade (or its completed
capabilities), and no selection opens the general Defenders/Thieves guide.

Use **Target** to change which eligible thief a defender prefers: near the
bananas, near the exit, carrying the most, or strongest. Not every defender can
hit flying thieves; check its information when Parrots arrive.

## Why can't I buy Darrilla in Adventure?

Commons offers nineteen ordinary defenders. The special Darrilla character is
not available for purchase in this level. Try Darrilla in **Workshop**, which
provides test resources and a separate saved session for experimenting with the
twenty-character roster. Workshop does not award Adventure results.

## How do I pause, change speed or turn off sound?

During a round, **Play/Pause** freezes or resumes the action. Between rounds,
Play begins the next one. The adjacent speed control cycles from **1× to 4×**.
Open the **gear** for Settings, including **Sound** and **Calm motion**. Calm
motion reduces decorative movement while the game continues. Settings also lets
you reopen the optional instructions or save and return to the title.

## Where is my progress saved?

Adventure and Workshop use separate local saves. The game saves at key actions,
round changes, periodically during play, and when sent to the background.
**Resume** returns to the saved Adventure. Sound and Calm preferences are also
stored on the device; there is no account-based cloud sync.

If the game reports unreadable or incompatible data, stop and contact us with
the message shown. **Do not uninstall, clear app data or replace save files to
troubleshoot.** Development updates may keep an older run separately when its
level content is incompatible with a newer version.

## Where can I download it?

The [Android closed-test page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.bananarilla)
is configured for the invited ArrowZilla tester lists. The first release is
being prepared and has not been submitted for review, so installation is not
available yet. Once approved and released, use the invited Google account and
follow the opt-in page's download link. [Enrollment help](/testing/).

iPhone/iPad TestFlight setup is in progress for the matching ArrowZilla testers.
External access will require Apple beta review, which has not been submitted.
There is no public enrollment link. Keep existing saves when updating; contact
us rather than uninstalling if an earlier local build has a different signer.
