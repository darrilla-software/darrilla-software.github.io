Last updated: October 23, 2026

FuzzRilla is a puzzle game made by Darrilla Software. This policy describes
the current Android development build, version **0.2.1**.

## Playing the game

FuzzRilla does not require an account or ask for your name, email address,
contacts or location to play. The current build works offline and does not
send gameplay progress or usage data to Darrilla Software.

The current build has no advertising, in-app purchases or analytics SDKs.
Unity's connected analytics, advertising and cloud diagnostic services are
disabled in the project.

## Data stored on your device

The game stores your current level, board arrangement, collection totals,
moves, score and refill state locally so you can resume a settled turn.
Sound, motion and appearance preferences are also stored locally. Saves and
best scores from the earlier endless prototype are retained separately on
upgrade; they are not uploaded to Darrilla Software.

The game does not provide its own account or cloud-sync service. Your phone's
backup settings may handle app data separately. Clearing app data or removing
the app can remove your local saves and preferences.

## Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose
to send, such as a device model, screenshot or bug description. We use it to
answer your request and investigate the issue, and retain it as needed for
those purposes. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive
information. Contact us about information you have sent directly to us.

## Future versions

If a future version adds services that change how information is handled,
we will update this page to describe them and revise the date. The presence
of advertising or purchases in another Darrilla game does not mean they are
included in this FuzzRilla build.

## Contact

For privacy questions or requests about support information, email
[darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=FuzzRilla%20privacy).
