FuzzRilla is in development. This page describes Android development version
**0.2.1**, with ten jungle levels.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=FuzzRilla%20support)
with questions, bug reports or feedback. Include your device model, Android
version, game version, level number and what happened. A screenshot or short
recording can help; leave out private information.

## How do I make a match?

Drag a whole row horizontally or a whole column vertically. You can move more
than one space, and pieces wrap around the edge. Three or more of the same Rilla
connected horizontally or vertically will clear; L and square shapes count.
Diagonal neighbors and pieces on opposite edges do not join for matching.

Use their faces, ears and hairstyles to recognize matching characters. Color
is an additional clue.

## What am I trying to collect?

The portraits, names and counts above the board show the level's targets.
Every target must be complete to win. Other matches still score and rearrange
the board, but only the relevant character clears advance each target.

## What uses a move?

A successful slide uses one move, including every cascade it triggers.
A slide that makes no match returns without spending a move. Complete the
goals during the last move's cascade and you still win. If any goal remains
when that turn ends and no moves remain, the game offers Retry.

Version 0.2.1 doubled the earlier move allowances to **6–28 moves**, depending
on the level. An existing attempt keeps its progress and receives the larger
allowance when resumed.

## Where are Hint and Shuffle?

The chapter has no Hint or manual Shuffle buttons. If no legal move remains,
the game announces an automatic rearrangement that preserves the crew already
on the board. It grants no moves, score or collection credit. If a playable
arrangement cannot be found, the attempt ends with a Retry option.

## What does Retry do?

Retry restarts the current level with its full move allowance and empty
collection counts. It uses the same starting board and refill sequence, so you
can try a different approach. During play, Retry asks for confirmation first.

## What happens after level ten?

The game shows **Chapter complete**. You can replay the final level or start
the chapter again. Level eleven and later content are not in this build.

## Where is my progress saved?

The current level, board, collection totals, moves, score and settings are stored
on your device. The game saves after a turn settles. If the app closes during
an animation, it returns to the last saved turn. Finished win/loss results are
also restored, so closing the app does not skip a result.

The campaign does not provide account-based cloud sync. Clearing app data or
removing the app can remove progress. Old endless-prototype saves and best
scores are retained separately when upgrading; they do not become campaign progress.

## Can I reduce motion or change the appearance?

Open **?** for settings. **Calm motion** stops the forest sway and reduces match
effects. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow phone** for the interface;
the forest and character artwork keep their normal brightness. Sound can be
turned off there too.

Board characters stay still until matching. In **Meet the Crew**, select a
character with a small portrait, then tap its large picture to animate it once.

## Where can I download it?

There is no public store download yet. See the
[FuzzRilla overview](/fuzzrilla/) for current availability, or email us about testing.
