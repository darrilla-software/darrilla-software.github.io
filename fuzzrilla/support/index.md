FuzzRilla is in beta testing. This page describes Android and iPhone/iPad version
**0.4.6 (17)**, the 100-level tester update. Store processing and review may
delay availability for invited testers.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=FuzzRilla%20support)
with questions, bug reports or feedback. Include your device model, operating-system
version, game version, level number and what happened. A screenshot or short
recording can help; leave out private information.

## How do I make a match?

Drag a whole row horizontally or a whole column vertically. You can move more
than one space, and pieces wrap around the edge. Three or more of the same Rilla
connected horizontally or vertically will clear; L and square shapes count.
Diagonal neighbors and pieces on opposite edges do not join for matching.

Use their faces, ears and hairstyles to recognize matching characters. Color
is an additional clue.

## What am I trying to collect?

The portraits, names and counts above the board show the level's targets.
Every collection target must be complete to win. When present, clear every
leafy patch and free every frozen Rilla too. Making or using powers is optional.
Other matches still score and rearrange the board, but only the relevant character clears advance each target.

## What uses a move?

A successful slide uses one move, including every cascade it triggers.
A slide that makes no match returns without spending a move. Complete the
goals during the last move's cascade and you still win. If any goal remains
when that turn ends and no moves remain, the game offers Retry.

Move budgets range from 8 to 30. All levels from 21 onward allow 30 moves.

## Where are Hint and Shuffle?

The chapter has no Hint or manual Shuffle buttons. If no legal move remains,
the game announces an automatic rearrangement that preserves the crew already
on the board. It grants no moves, score or collection credit. If a playable
arrangement cannot be found, the attempt ends with a Retry option.

## What does Retry do?

Retry restarts the current level with its full move allowance and empty
collection counts. It uses the same starting board and refill sequence, so you
can try a different approach. During play, Retry asks for confirmation first.

## What are drums and overgrown ground?

Starting at level 11, a player-made match of five or more can create a drum.
A row slide places it at the leftmost matched space on that row; a column slide
places it at the topmost matched space on that column. A gold outline shows the
spot before the drum falls with its Rilla. Match that Rilla again to activate it:
↔ clears its current row, and ↕ clears its current column. Drums hit also activate.

The **Ground** goal asks you to clear particular overgrown spaces. Match the
Rilla standing on a leafy patch, or hit that space with a drum. Plants stay on
their spaces when Rillas move. They do not block slides, and sliding through
alone does not clear them. Ground begins at level 21. Level 31 introduces Double Chest Pound.

## What are Double Chest Pound, Boom and Lightning?

From level 31, a player-made T or cross of six or more plain Rillas can create
**Double Chest Pound** (+). Match its Rilla again to clear both its current row
and column. The matching group must lie on one row and column with at least
three arms at their junction.

From level 41, a compact connected group of six or more identical plain Rillas
inside a 3×3 area can make **Rilla Boom**. Match the token to clear the surrounding
3×3 area. The blast stops at board edges instead of wrapping.

From level 81, a connected group of eight or more identical plain Rillas makes
**Rilla Lightning**, taking priority over the other power shapes. Its large golden
bolt marks the Rilla: match it again to hit every Rilla of that kind across the board.

Only the player's initial match creates new powers. Cascades can activate powers,
and powers hit by other powers also activate. Newly created tokens survive their
creation wave and fall with their Rilla. All powers remain optional.

## How do I free Frozen Rillas?

From level 51, chunky ice surrounds mark trapped Rillas. Each frozen Rilla locks
**both its row and its column**. Use other lines to match it with its own kind;
that frees and collects it.

A drum, double, Boom or Lightning hit only breaks the ice. The Rilla stays on the
board, earns no collection credit, and leaves any ground beneath it intact.
Adjacent or diagonal matches do not thaw ice.

Frozen Rillas stay fixed during gravity and automatic rearrangements. Other
Rillas fall past their cells in column order. Once thawed, a Rilla falls normally.
Free every frozen Rilla before finishing the level.

## When does the board get bigger?

Levels 1–70 use 6×6 boards. Levels 71–100 use **8×8 boards** in the same screen
area. Whole-line sliding, wrapping and connected matching work as before.

## Why does the result include bonuses?

Finishing early earns 500 points per unused move. Each target Rilla above quota
earns another 100 points, including extras in the final cascade. These bonuses
are added to match/chain points. Goals retain actual totals such as 7/4; results
show the breakdown. A loss receives no unused-move bonus.

## What happens after level 100?

The game shows **Chapter complete**. You can replay the final level or start
the chapter again. Earlier chapter completions can continue into the added levels
without resetting overall scores or statistics.

## Where is my progress saved?

The current level, board, collection totals, moves, score and settings are stored
on your device. The game saves after a turn settles. If the app closes during
an animation, it returns to the last saved turn. Finished win/loss results are
also restored, so closing the app does not skip a result. Overall score adds each
level's best winning score; My progress retains lifetime collections, moves, chains,
powers and ground cleared across attempts. Updates preserve existing attempts and
expand the score history for added levels.

The campaign does not provide account-based cloud sync. Clearing app data or
removing the app can remove progress. Old endless-prototype saves and best
scores are retained separately when upgrading; they do not become campaign progress.

## Can I reduce motion or change the appearance?

Open **?** for settings. **Calm motion** stops the forest sway and reduces match
effects. Choose **Light**, **Dark** or **Follow phone** for the interface;
the forest and character artwork keep their normal brightness. Follow phone
currently reads Android system appearance; use Light/Dark on iPhone/iPad. Sound can be
turned off there too.

Board characters stay still until matching. In **Meet the Crew**, select a
character with a small portrait, then tap its large picture to animate it once.

## Can testers jump to another level?

Tester builds include **? → Test levels…**, also available through **My progress**.
Enter 1–100 and choose **Go to level**. This starts a fresh attempt using the
current rules and replaces the attempt in progress; overall scores, lifetime
statistics and settings stay. Skipped levels receive no credit. Cancel leaves
the attempt alone. Restarting from level 1 is a separate, confirmed reset.
