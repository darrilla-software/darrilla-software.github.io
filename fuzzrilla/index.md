## A whole line. A new possibility.

Every slide moves an entire row or column. Pieces wrap around the board's
edges, opening up matches you might miss at first glance. Bring three or more
of the same Rilla together in a connected group; L shapes count too.

Matching Rillas beat their chests and clear the board. The crew falls into
place, new faces arrive, and one match can tumble into another. Listen for
the pop rising in pitch as a chain grows.

## A jungle chapter to beat

The current Android development build has **ten designed levels**. Each has
its own starting board, collection targets and move budget. Collect the
pictured Rillas before your moves run out.

- **Choose your targets.** A useful match helps fill a collection goal or sets up your next move.
- **Make cascades count.** A successful slide costs one move; its chain reactions cost no extra moves.
- **Finish on the last move.** Complete every goal during that turn and the level is yours.
- **Try another approach.** Retry repeats the same starting puzzle and refill sequence.

There are no Hint or manual Shuffle buttons. If the board has no legal moves,
the game announces a rearrangement of the same crew, without adding moves or
collection credit. Level ten finishes the chapter, with the option to replay.

## Seven faces. Seven personalities.

Sunny is the cheerful showoff. Bash is the shy sweetheart. Rex brings a
competitive streak, while Moss takes things slowly. Gus is a lovable grump,
Vivi loves the spotlight, and Zip is always up to something.

Different faces, ears and hairstyles help identify each Rilla; color is an
extra clue. There are no letter badges. The board stays still between moves,
and matching characters react through changing poses. In **Meet the Crew**,
tap a character's large picture to see its animation.

## Play at your own pace

There is no timer. A softly moving forest sits above the board, with a Calm
motion setting to stop the wind and reduce effects. Choose Light, Dark or
Follow phone; the artwork keeps its brightness in either theme. Progress and
settings are saved on your device, and gameplay works offline.

## Follow FuzzRilla

FuzzRilla is in development for Android. It is **not yet available as a public
Google Play or App Store download**. This page describes the current development
build; features and level balance may change as testing continues.

More levels, earned powers and new objectives are being planned. They are not
part of the current ten-level chapter, and no release date is announced.
