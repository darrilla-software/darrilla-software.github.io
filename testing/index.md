## How to join

1. Open your game's link while signed in with the **Google account invited to
   that game's test**. Use the same account in Google Play on your Android device.
2. Choose **Become a tester** on Google's page, then follow its Google Play link
   to install or update the game.
3. If the game says you are not eligible, check the account first. Then
   [email Darrilla](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=Darrilla%20Android%20testing)
   with the game name and the Google account you want invited. Do not send a
   password or post account details publicly.
4. If you already joined that game's **internal** test, leave the internal test
   before joining its closed test. Updates can take time to appear in Google Play.

Use Google Play to update an existing installation. Do not uninstall or clear
app data to solve a testing-link problem: doing so can remove local progress.
Store availability depends on your account, device, country and the active release.
See [Google's testing guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9845334)
for the platform's enrollment process.

## What feedback helps?

Tell us the game, app version, device and operating-system version, what you
expected and what happened. Include the level or time control when relevant.
A screenshot or short recording helps; leave out private information.
For Chess Clock, try timing, handoff, sound and interruptions before relying on
a test build for a match. For puzzles, tell us where controls, goals or difficulty
were confusing. ArrowZilla tester builds use Google demo ads; FuzzRilla has no ads.

## iPhone and iPad

ArrowZilla, FuzzRilla and MonkeyRilla testing uses invited TestFlight access. Contact us about
an invitation; there is no public TestFlight enrollment link on this site.
[Darrilla Chess Clock](/chess-clock/) also has a public App Store listing.

MonkeyRilla’s first external TestFlight build is waiting for Apple’s beta review.
Existing invited internal testers can use their personal TestFlight invitation.
A personal invitation is not a public enrollment link.
