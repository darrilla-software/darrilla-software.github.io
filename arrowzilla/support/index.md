ArrowZilla is in beta testing. This page covers the current game. Android testers
can [join the Google Play closed test](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.arrowzilla)
with an invited Google account; the [testing guide](/testing/) explains access.

## Contact Darrilla

Email [darrilla@gmail.com](mailto:darrilla@gmail.com?subject=ArrowZilla%20support)
with questions, bug reports, or feedback.

For an issue, include your device model, operating system and build version,
the level number or picture name, and what happened. A screenshot or short
recording helps us understand arrow selection and reveal problems. Leave out
private information.

## Why won't an arrow leave?

Another arrow's head or tail may cross its exit path. Clear the blocker first. Hold
near the arrow's head to see its exit ray: green means clear; red means blocked.
Hold it again to hide that ray.

## How do I inspect a crowded board?

Pinch to zoom and drag with two fingers to pan. Then tap the arrow you want
to release. Holding near a head previews its path without releasing it.

## What does Zap do?

**Zap** releases every arrow that is currently clear in one batch. Arrows freed
by that batch wait for another Zap or tap.

## How do stars work?

A completed level earns three stars with no blocked attempts. A small number
of blocked attempts earns two; more earns one. The game saves your last and
best completed ratings. Restart a level to try for a cleaner run.

## Where is my progress saved?

The game saves your selected level, unlocked levels, and ratings
on your device. It does not provide account-based cloud sync. Clearing app data
or removing the app can remove that progress.

## Why did a level lose its rating after an update?

Some retired puzzles have been replaced with picture levels. A replacement is
an unsolved puzzle and must be played to earn stars and a time. Ratings for
retained puzzles stay with those puzzles as their level numbers change.

## What are the Daily challenges?

Tap **Daily** to open the daily hub. It has four puzzles that unlock in order,
alternating timed and three-heart challenges. The main button starts, resumes
or retries your next puzzle. Progress stays separate from the campaign.
Complete all four to earn the displayed daily reward. A missed day resets the
streak. **Normal puzzles** returns to your saved campaign.

## What happens when I fail a timed or heart challenge?

Retry starts the same puzzle without recording a completion. When a rewarded
ad is available, the restart or skip flow waits for its reward and completed
close; cancelling early does not grant the action. If an ad is unavailable or
fails, the game provides the relevant fallback. Skipped campaign puzzles remain
incomplete in Levels. Leaving a failed Daily does not skip it or erase earlier
clears from that day.

## How do hints, Zap rewards and ads work?

An earned Hint or Zap is banked for its next button press; it should not fire
just because the ad closed. Reward offers show whether an ad is available or
a free fallback can be claimed. Closed-test and TestFlight builds use Google's
**demo/test ads**. You do not need to click an advertisement to earn a reward.

Where available, the one-time ad-removal purchase removes automatic between-level
ads. Optional rewarded ads remain. Check the store's confirmation and displayed
price before buying, including while testing.

## Where are sound and privacy controls?

Open **Pause → Settings** for the installed build's sound, display and privacy
options. **Ad privacy choices** appears when Google's consent service requires
it; the **Privacy policy** link opens this game's policy. Settings labels and
available audio controls can vary between tester updates.

## Where can I download it?

[Join Android closed testing](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.darrilla.arrowzilla)
with your invited Google account. If access fails, use the [testing guide](/testing/).
iPhone/iPad testing is by TestFlight invitation; see the
[ArrowZilla overview](/arrowzilla/) or contact us.
